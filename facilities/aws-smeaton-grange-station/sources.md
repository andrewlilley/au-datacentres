# Sources: aws-smeaton-grange-station

Access date for all: 2026-09-27. Sources 1-5 were first recorded from WebSearch extracts when direct fetching was blocked and remain snippet-only. In the 2026-09-27 gap-filling pass the NSW planning portal pages (6, 9, 10) and the SEARs (8) were fetched and read directly; the withdrawal and SSD number are now verified on the portal.

1. https://baxtel.com/data-centers/amazon-aws - Baxtel: AWS data centre with 40 MW IT, second facility in Smeaton Grange Park; land on Turner Road bought Mar 2022 for AU$30.18m.
2. https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/amazon-withdraws-plan-for-40mw-data-center-in-sydney-australia/ - AWS sought SEARs for a 40 MW data centre at 52 Turner Road, Smeaton Grange (~AU$50m); application withdrawn by Arup.
3. https://www.theurbandeveloper.com/articles/amazon-lays-out-south-sydney-data-centre-plans - initial proposal at 52 Turner Road, Smeaton Grange cancelled Nov 2024; Amazon moving to 43-61 Turner Road, Gregory Hills (9.4 ha); early works DA to Camden Council ~$29m.
4. https://www.itnews.com.au/news/aws-linked-to-fresh-450m-sydney-data-centre-build-606318 - AWS-linked $450m data centre at 43-61 Turner Road, Gregory Hills, 53 MW; ~2 km from existing AWS facility at 42A Bluett Drive, Smeaton Grange.
5. https://majorprojects.planningportal.nsw.gov.au/prweb/PRRestService/mp/01/getContent?AttachRef=SSD-68013714%2120250207T055130.541+GMT - EIS document for SSD-68013714 data centre at 43-61 Turner Road, Gregory Hills NSW 2557; 53 MW; construction Q1 2026 to Q2 2027, fully operational Q2 2028 (per search extract).
6. https://www.planningportal.nsw.gov.au/major-projects/projects/43-61-turner-road-data-centre - portal project page for 43-61 Turner Road Data Centre (SSD-68013714, Camden), fetched: Decision Approved, Determination Date 21/11/2025, Decider Director; 'power consumption capacity of 53 megawatts (MW)'; Modification 1 - Substation layout changes, last modified 26/06/2026 (context only - successor site).
7. https://portal.spatial.nsw.gov.au/server/rest/services/NSW_Geocoded_Addressing_Theme/FeatureServer/1 - accessed 2026-09-27 - NSW Spatial Services Geocoded Addressing Theme (GNAF-based address points), queried address = '52 TURNER ROAD SMEATON GRANGE'; supports lat/lon.
8. https://majorprojects.planningportal.nsw.gov.au/prweb/PRRestService/mp/01/getContent?AttachRef=SSD-60185233%2120230719T232129.351+GMT - Industry Specific SEARs for SSD-60185233 '52 Turner Road Data Centre', Location 1/DP603134 within Camden, Applicant ARUP PTY LIMITED, Date of issue 20/07/2023.
9. https://www.planningportal.nsw.gov.au/major-projects/projects/52-turner-road-data-centre - portal project page, fetched: SSD-60185233, 'Current Status: Withdrawn'; 'Construction and operation of a data centre with a capacity of 40 MW including emergency back-up generators, cooling plant, diesel and lithium ion battery storage, substation and associated infrastructure'; only document is the SEARs.
10. https://majorprojects.planningportal.nsw.gov.au/prweb/PRRestService/mp/01/getContent?AttachRef=SSD-68013714%2120251121T045240.855+GMT and https://majorprojects.planningportal.nsw.gov.au/prweb/PRRestService/mp/01/getContent?AttachRef=SSD-68013714-PA-2%2120260310T234929.063+GMT - (successor site, context only) SSD-68013714 Notice of Decision: consent granted 21 November 2025, applicant ARUP PTY LIMITED, 'capital investment of $800 million and the creation of up to 100 construction jobs and 50 operational jobs'; A9/A10 notice dated 11 March 2026: anticipated commencement of construction 17 April 2026, not staged, building 'with an operational capacity of 61.7 MW'.
