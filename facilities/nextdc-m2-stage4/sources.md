# Sources: nextdc-m2-stage4

NEXTDC M2 Stage 4 (Kapitol, new 5-level building, six data halls). Split from nextdc-m2 on 2026-09-27.

Access date for all: 2026-09-27. Sources marked "(snippet only)" were seen only in web-search result snippets; all others were read in full (PDFs as extracted text).

1. Kapitol Group, 'NEXTDC M2 Stage 4 Data Centre' case study (published 2022-06-08). https://www.kapitol.com/our-projects/industrial/nextdc-m2-stage-4-data-centre (accessed 2026-09-27). Supports: Builder Kapitol; architect VIA Architects; status Complete; new building of six data halls over 5 levels 'including a 10-megawatt data hall'; 'the building was constructed to have an IT cooling capacity of 18 megawatts, equating to 6 megawatts for each data hall'; 10,000m2 technical floor space; 110,583 hours worked; 100 specialist electricians; 2022 MBAV Excellence in Construction of Industrial Buildings award; connected to operational Stage 1 via a new corridor.
2. Kapitol Group, 'NEXTDC M2 Data Centre Portfolio'. https://www.kapitol.com/our-projects/industrial/nextdc-m2-data-centre-portfolio-2 (accessed 2026-09-27). Supports: Kapitol is builder of M2 Stages 2-6. Stage 2: 'a 7-month base build and fit out programme which added an additional 6MW to M2's 60MW facility ... two levels, two data halls, and 15 switch rooms ... constructed adjoining to and integrating with Stage 1 ... delivered one month ahead of programme in January 2019'. Stage 3: 7,494m2 'Innovation Tower' offices, completed June 2023 (no IT capacity). Stage 4: new building, six data halls over 5 levels, 'including a 10-megawatt data hall'. Stage 5: 'delivered in a tight, 11-month programme ... five data halls ... added an additional 3750m2 of technical space and 15MW to the 60MW M2 facility'; 'M2 stage 5 reached practical completion in March 2022'. 'Stage 5 Expansion Works': design and build of several data halls (fit-out) 'due for completion in July 2024' (MW not stated). Stage 6: 'will add 8MW of new capacity ... Currently under construction, the project is set for completion in 2025'. (Page fetched with curl; WebFetch was refused with 403.)
