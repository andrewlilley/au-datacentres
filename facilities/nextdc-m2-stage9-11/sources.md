6. Kapitol Group, 'NEXTDC M2 Data Centre Portfolio'. https://www.kapitol.com/our-projects/industrial/nextdc-m2-data-centre-portfolio-2 (accessed 2026-09-27). Supports: Kapitol is builder of M2 Stages 2-6. Stage 2: 'a 7-month base build and fit out programme which added an additional 6MW to M2's 60MW facility ... two levels, two data halls, and 15 switch rooms ... constructed adjoining to and integrating with Stage 1 ... delivered one month ahead of programme in January 2019'. Stage 3: 7,494m2 'Innovation Tower' offices, completed June 2023 (no IT capacity). Stage 4: new building, six data halls over 5 levels, 'including a 10-megawatt data hall'. Stage 5: 'delivered in a tight, 11-month programme ... five data halls ... added an additional 3750m2 of technical space and 15MW to the 60MW M2 facility'; 'M2 stage 5 reached practical completion in March 2022'. 'Stage 5 Expansion Works': design and build of several data halls (fit-out) 'due for completion in July 2024' (MW not stated). Stage 6: 'will add 8MW of new capacity ... Currently under construction, the project is set for completion in 2025'. (Page fetched with curl; WebFetch was refused with 403.)
