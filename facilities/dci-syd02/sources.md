# Sources: dci-syd02

Note: in this session the egress proxy blocked all direct page fetches (planningportal.nsw.gov.au, itnews, DCD, multiplex.global, nominatim etc.). Facts below were taken from WebSearch result snippets/summaries of the listed pages, not from reading the pages in full. Verify in Phase 4.

1. https://dcidatacenters.com/news/dci-secures-development-approval-for-new-sydney-data-centre/ (accessed 2026-09-27) - DCI press release: Development Approval from Blacktown City Council for new 36 MW data centre (SYD02) at Eastern Creek; AU$400m; service ready Q4 2022; developer DCI.
2. https://www.itnews.com.au/news/dci-lands-approval-to-build-400m-second-sydney-data-centre-559599 (accessed 2026-09-27) - Blacktown City Council approved 36 MW SYD02, $400m, same site as 11 MW SYD01; ready Q4 2022; DCI owned by Brookfield.
3. https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/dci-build-au400-million-sydney-data-center/ (accessed 2026-09-27) - AU$400m, 36 MW, Q4 2022 target; DCI aims for 65 MW combined in Sydney.
4. https://www.multiplex.global/projects/dci-syd02-enabling-works/ (accessed 2026-09-27) - Multiplex early enabling works at DCI SYD02 (fencing, demolition of four industrial buildings, stormwater tanks, relocating SYD01 water storage). Main works contractor not stated.
5. https://www.planningalerts.org.au/applications/1997501 (accessed 2026-09-27) - Application at 90 Peter Brock Drive, Eastern Creek NSW 2766, dated 17 Jun 2021 (content not read; supports postcode).
6. https://www.planningportal.nsw.gov.au/major-projects/projects/eastern-creek-data-centre-expansion-project (accessed 2026-09-27, via search snippet) - SSD-21342738 at 90 Peter Brock Drive, applicant NineZero DC Sub TC I Pty Ltd; some listings (datacenters.com, baxtel) describe SSD approval covering "SYD02/SYD03" ~36 MW.
7. https://www.ttwengineers.com/projects/dci-eastern-creek-eastern-creek-nsw (accessed 2026-09-27, via search snippet) - describes "new 7 storey 36MW data centre comprising up to 12 data halls" at DCI Eastern Creek.
8. https://datacenterhawk.com/marketplace/providers/dci-data-centers/90-peter-brock-drive/syd02 (from master.csv; not accessible this session) - lists SYD02 at 90 Peter Brock Drive.
