# Sources: nextdc-m2

Access date for all: 2026-09-27. Sources marked "(snippet only)" were seen only in web-search result snippets; all others were read in full.

1. iTnews, 'NextDC picks Tullamarine for second Melbourne data centre' (19 May 2016). https://www.itnews.com.au/news/nextdc-picks-tullamarine-for-second-melbourne-data-centre-419737 (accessed 2026-09-27). Supports: developer NEXTDC; $85 million; 2MW initial, 25MW planned IT load; completion expected 2H FY2017; up to 200 direct jobs plus ~100 supply chain.
2. The Urban Developer, 'NextDC Site Shopping Spree Totals $160million In NSW, Vic' (23 May 2016). https://www.theurbandeveloper.com/articles/nextdc-site-shopping-spree-totals-160m-in-nsw-vic (accessed 2026-09-27). Supports: AU$85m for land, base building and infrastructure; 2MW initial, 25MW full fit-out; 'up to 200 jobs on and off-site, with another 100 jobs supported deeper in the supply chain'; 12-18 months construction.
3. NEXTDC FY17 Appendix 4E and Annual Report (Aug 2017). https://www.annualreports.com/HostedData/AnnualReportArchive/N/ASX_NXT_2017.pdf (accessed 2026-09-27). Supports (p13): 'an increase in the target capacity of M2 by 15MW to 40MW and B2 by 6MW to 12MW'; (p24) B2 and M2 'an investment of over $100 million so far to have the facilities customer ready in FY18'; (p35) development began on M2 and B2 during FY17.
