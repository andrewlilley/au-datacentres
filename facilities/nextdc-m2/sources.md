# Sources: nextdc-m2

Access date for all: 2026-09-27. Sources marked "(snippet only)" were seen only in web-search result snippets; all others were read in full.

1. iTnews, 'NextDC picks Tullamarine for second Melbourne data centre' (19 May 2016). https://www.itnews.com.au/news/nextdc-picks-tullamarine-for-second-melbourne-data-centre-419737 (accessed 2026-09-27). Supports: developer NEXTDC; $85 million; 2MW initial, 25MW planned IT load; completion expected 2H FY2017; up to 200 direct jobs plus ~100 supply chain.
2. The Urban Developer, 'NextDC Site Shopping Spree Totals $160million In NSW, Vic' (23 May 2016). https://www.theurbandeveloper.com/articles/nextdc-site-shopping-spree-totals-160m-in-nsw-vic (accessed 2026-09-27). Supports: AU$85m for land, base building and infrastructure; 2MW initial, 25MW full fit-out; 'up to 200 jobs on and off-site, with another 100 jobs supported deeper in the supply chain'; 12-18 months construction.
3. NEXTDC FY17 Appendix 4E and Annual Report (Aug 2017). https://www.annualreports.com/HostedData/AnnualReportArchive/N/ASX_NXT_2017.pdf (accessed 2026-09-27). Supports (p13): 'an increase in the target capacity of M2 by 15MW to 40MW and B2 by 6MW to 12MW'; (p24) B2 and M2 'an investment of over $100 million so far to have the facilities customer ready in FY18'; (p35) development began on M2 and B2 during FY17.
4. FDC Construction & Fitout, 'NEXTDC M2' project page. https://www.fdcbuilding.com.au/projects/nextdc-m2/ (accessed 2026-09-27). Supports: builder; 40,000 sqm land, 6,000 sqm building; remediation of 32,500 sqm site; Data Hall 1; Tier IV designed and constructed.
5. PeeringDB facility 4843 'NEXTDC M2' (API). https://www.peeringdb.com/fac/4843 (accessed 2026-09-27). Supports: 75 Sharps Rd, Tullamarine 3043; lat/lon cross-check.
6. Kapitol Group, 'NEXTDC M2 Data Centre Portfolio'. https://www.kapitol.com/our-projects/industrial/nextdc-m2-data-centre-portfolio-2 (accessed 2026-09-27). Supports: Kapitol built Stage 2 (6MW, Jan 2019, 'adjoining to and integrating with Stage 1'), Stage 3 office tower, Stage 4, Stage 5 (15MW, Mar 2022), Stage 6 (8MW); these stages are now separate rows (nextdc-m2-stage2, -stage4, -stage5, -stage6).
7. NEXTDC, 'M2 Melbourne Data Centre' page. https://www.nextdc.com/data-centres/melbourne-data-centres/m2-melbourne (accessed 2026-09-27). Supports: now 120MW+ Tier IV facility in Tullamarine.
8. NEXTDC, 'Our History'. https://www.nextdc.com/about-us/our-history (accessed 2026-09-27). Supports: May 2016 acquired properties for B2 and M2; November 2017 M2 opened as Victoria's first UTI Tier IV certified data centre.
9. CDL Construction, 'Next DC - M2 Data Centre Hall, Sharps Road & Barrie Road, Tullamarine'. https://www.cdl.net.au/m2-data-centre-hall-sharps-road-tullamarine/ (accessed 2026-09-27). Supports: site frontage to Sharps Rd and Barrie Rd; later works for head contractor Kapitol.
10. Esri ArcGIS World Geocoder, findAddressCandidates for '75 Sharps Road, Tullamarine VIC 3043' (queried 2026-09-27). https://geocode.arcgis.com/arcgis/rest/services/World/GeocodeServer/findAddressCandidates (accessed 2026-09-27). Supports: lat/lon (PointAddress, score 100).
11. NEXTDC FY20 Full-Year Results presentation (27 Aug 2020). https://www.nextdc.com/hubfs/ASX%20Announcements/2104835.pdf (accessed 2026-09-27). Supports: M2 'Installed capacity 10MW', 'Total IT capacity 60MW' at 30 Jun 2020 (used to derive ~4MW for Stage 1 = 10MW minus Stage 2's 6MW).
12. NEXTDC FY19 Annual Report (Aug 2019). https://www.annualreports.com/HostedData/AnnualReportArchive/N/ASX_NXT_2019.pdf (accessed 2026-09-27). Supports: 'third data hall at M2 Melbourne' completed in FY19 (Stage 2, two halls), implying one data hall in Stage 1.
13. Brimbank City Council, Public Register of Planning Applications (data as at 22/07/2025). https://serviceapi.brimbank.vic.gov.au/CMServiceAPI/Record/6580992/file/document (accessed 2026-09-27). Supports: planning authority Brimbank City Council; data centre permit P589/2016 at 75 Sharps Rd (Pt 5 extension amendment issued 17/01/2024; Pt 6 lapsed 10/04/2025; Pt 7 under consideration, received 23/04/2025, also covering 9 Barrie Rd).
14. PlanningAlerts, '75 Sharps Road Tullamarine, VIC' application P341/2022. https://www.planningalerts.org.au/applications/2601583 (accessed 2026-09-27). Supports: Brimbank City Council is the planning authority for 75 Sharps Road.
