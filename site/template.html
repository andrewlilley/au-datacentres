Australian Data Centre Register

Every data centre building or phase of 5 MW or more completed in Australia since January 2016, under construction, or holding planning approval, plus the lodged and announced pipeline. Each figure links back to its source: planning assessments, EIS documents, operator releases and contractor records.

Capacity is design IT capacity at full build for each building or phase, as stated in planning or operator documents. It is not the load energised today, which for part-built campuses can be far lower. Totals exclude withdrawn projects and rows with no published figure. Lodged and announced projects (hollow dots, off by default) carry the headline figure from the announcement or application, often a whole campus or grid connection, and have not had per-facility research.

Dot size scales with design MW

Jobs

Planning documents report construction jobs on different bases. Each figure is tagged: peak on-site workforce, avg workforce over the build, FTE-yrs, total headcount, or n/s where the source gives no definition. Only like-for-like figures should be added together.

Scope

One row per building or phase. Completed facilities from 1 January 2016. Lodged means a planning application or SEARs request is in the system but undetermined; announced means a public announcement or land acquisition with no application found. Status reflects the latest source found.

Data

Source data, per-facility records and method: github.com/andrewlilley/au-datacentres. Coastline from Natural Earth (public domain). Locations are geocoded address points, not building footprints.
